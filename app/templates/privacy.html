Privacy Policy

Short version: there is no account, and your data stays in your browser.

What we store

{{APP_NAME}} saves your settings, personal best, your last tests and your saved words in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share a score.

What the server sees

When a test ends, your keystroke timings and the words you typed are sent to our server once to calculate your detailed results. They are not linked to you, not saved and not sold. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, for a short time.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

Sharing

If you use a share button, the social network you choose receives the text you share. Their own privacy policy applies.

Removing your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to delete everything. There is nothing to delete on our side.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick your test

    Choose a level, a mode and a length from the bar above the text. One minute is the most popular.

  2. 2

    Meet the word

    The card under the text shows the word, how it sounds, its plain-English meaning and its Bangla meaning.

  3. 3

    Type a real sentence

    Just start typing. The timer starts on your first key, and each sentence uses the word in context.

  4. 4

    Review and practice

    See your WPM, accuracy and problem keys, then save new words and practice them again later.

Modes and levels

Learn words

You type full example sentences. The vocabulary word is underlined in the text, and the card explains it while you type.

Speed only

You type a stream of single vocabulary words for pure speed. The card still shows each word, so you learn as you go.

Easy
Common everyday words, such as advantage or afraid.
Medium
Less common words you meet in news and books, such as abide or legitimacy.
Hard
Rarer but useful words, such as revamp or monolithic.

Levels come from how often a word appears in everyday English (the open wordfreq project).

Bangla meanings (বাংলা অর্থ)

Every word card shows up to three Bangla meanings for the part of speech used in the sentence you are typing. For example, abide as a verb shows বরদাস্ত করা, থাকা, প্রতীক্ষা করা.

The same meanings appear in your results and on My Words. They are machine translations from Google Translate's dictionary, made once when the word list was built, so check important words in a full dictionary.
